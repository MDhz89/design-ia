# Design principles

## Objective

Create product-led landing pages that feel intentionally designed instead of
generated from a generic SaaS template.

## Principles

1. Put the product before decoration.
2. Give every section one clear communication goal.
3. Prefer real product evidence over abstract marketing illustrations.
4. Use typography, spacing, rhythm, and composition as the main visual language.
5. Keep visual decisions consistent without making every section identical.
6. Use generous whitespace without creating empty or disconnected layouts.
7. Introduce asymmetry only when it improves hierarchy or storytelling.
8. Use motion to explain state, progression, or interaction—not as decoration.
9. Make important content understandable without relying on animation.
10. Remove elements that do not improve comprehension, trust, or conversion.

## Product presentation

- Show real interface screens whenever possible.
- Crop or annotate product views to explain a specific workflow.
- Do not place every screenshot inside a floating browser mockup.
- Explain the business problem before listing implementation details.
- Translate CRUD features into controlled processes and business outcomes.
- Never invent customers, testimonials, integrations, metrics, or capabilities.

## Composition

- Establish a deliberate type scale and spacing system before adding decoration.
- Vary section composition according to the content being explained.
- Prefer whitespace and subtle separators over unnecessary containers.
- Keep paragraphs readable and avoid overly wide text columns.
- Make the product visible early when product evidence is available.
- Use quiet borders, responsive states, and small transitions to make interfaces
  feel considered without adding visual noise.

## Content

- Write for the buyer and the real operational user.
- Use concrete language tied to the actual workflow.
- Make claims specific, supportable, and easy to verify.
- Use one primary conversion action consistently.
- Prefer clarity over cleverness.
